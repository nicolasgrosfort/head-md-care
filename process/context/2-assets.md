# Assets


Describes the list of elements necessary to build the the [scenes](scenes.md). The scene that will be prototyped is the first one: `Initial`.

The `Underworld` scene will be a teaser-like scene, to make people want to see what happens next, with a little preview (for example, just lighting the torches)?
