# Onboarding

## First seconds (before 1st interaction)

1. *When the visitor approach the iPad, what is the first thing they see ?*
    - The red string
    - Underworld ?
2. *Is there something standing out ?* 
   - The red string
1. *What is hidden?*
   - Who they are
   - Where they are
   - Why they are here
1. *Is there any sounds?*
   - Ambient environmental sound (Kankyo Ongaku ?)
1. *How does you world "feel"? What is the general mood?*
   - Magical
   - Mysterious
   - Calm
   - Vivid

## First Interaction

1. *What is the first action the user have to do?*
2. *How do they know what they should do?* 
3. *What happens if they don’t?*
4. *Do I show the user what to do? If yes, how?*
5. *Is there something glowing? A sound? something moving? A light? A color?*
6. *How does the user feel when they accomplished their first action?* 
7. *What happens next?*
8. *list the interactions that you will have to implement ( for eg. drag for X/to X)*
