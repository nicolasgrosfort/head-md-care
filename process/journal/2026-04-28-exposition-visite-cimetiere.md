# Key decisions

- **Scenario confirmed:** Cimetière de Plainpalais (Cimetière des Rois) is the chosen context.

# Key ideas

- **Kodokushi:** Japanese concept of dying alone, resonates personally with Nico. Connection between solitude, death, and care.
- **AI-generated game idea (early, not retained):** A game that partially generates itself through AI, representing the unpredictability of life — each choice has unforeseen consequences. Not pursued.
- **Cemetery / temple parallels:** Both are silent, clean, slow, respectful spaces with prominent nature. This parallel remains active throughout the project.
- **Poetry as care:** Sabrina's lecture — "poetry is the march of the world." Poetry is a form of negative capacity, of being a space-between. The object is not poetry, the process is. Poetry as a way of taking care of the world.
- **Apophenia / Pareidolia:** Concepts of how we project meaning onto patterns in space — relevant to how visitors read a cemetery.
