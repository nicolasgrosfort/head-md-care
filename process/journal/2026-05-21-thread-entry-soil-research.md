# Key decisions

- **Thread entry point defined:** The red thread exits from a grave. The player must clean the grave slightly to reveal it, then follows it underground.
- **Thread symbolism:** No definitive answer is needed — it symbolizes the cycle, life's loop, with a beginning and an end. Its ambiguity is its strength.

# Key ideas

- **Thread as narrative guide and moral compass:** Following the thread takes Hans (and the player) deeper — the fil d'Ariane inversé in action.
- **Six plants identified for the game world:** Research into specific plants for each spatial level (soil, surface, roots). Documented with visual references.
- **Soil research:** Research into what "dead soil" looks like versus living soil — relevant for the underground layer's visual design and for the earthworm/ver de terre concept.
