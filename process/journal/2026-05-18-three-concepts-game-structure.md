# Key decisions

- **Three core concepts finalized (with Janka):**
  1. **Présence** — attention as the instrument for reading the world
  2. **Cycle** — life/death as a loop independent of human will
  3. **Care (action and non-action)** — even inaction can be a form of care

- **Game structure locked: 4 levels × 4 seasons**
  - Level 1: Underworld (burial, decomposition)
  - Level 2: Soil/roots (underground connections)
  - Level 3: Earth surface (gravestones, gardener gestures)
  - Level 4: Space/sky (yew trees, birds, light)
  - Each season = one cycle through all 4 levels. Total: 16 scenes.

- **Three choices per scene:**
  1. Ne pas voir — not perceive
  2. Voir et agir — perceive and act
  3. Voir et ne pas agir — perceive and choose presence (wú wéi)

- **Kaïros as player identity:** The Greek god of the opportune moment — the right act at the right time.

# Key ideas

- **Central tension articulated:** We live in a cycle independent of our choices. Within it, two options: act or not act. Either can be care or its absence. Presence is the tool enabling discernment.
- **Choices have local and global impact:** Each scene-level choice ripples outward to affect the full seasonal cycle.
- **Hans's arc:** Learns to discern — to act or not act — based on sensitivity to the environment. Presence as the instrument for reading the world.
- **Ipomée (morning glory):** Plant introduced by Janka — opens for only 10 minutes each morning. A metaphor for the opportune moment (Kaïros).
