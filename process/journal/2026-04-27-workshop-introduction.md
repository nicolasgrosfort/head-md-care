# Key decisions

- **Scenario chosen:** The cemetery is the most compelling scenario. Nico ruled out the educational context and is drawn to Cimetière de Plainpalais (Cimetière des Rois).

# Key ideas

- **Presence as the core of care:** Presence is the first condition for any act of care — being available to the other, to what is happening. Sabrina introduces the concept of "negative capacity" (negative space, the poet as transparent).
- **Death as a gateway to life:** Being conscious of one's finitude brings one closer to truly inhabiting life. The cemetery is paradoxically a place that brings you closer to living.
- **Cynthia Fleury quote (defining autonomy):** "*One is autonomous when one feels the power to gather one's own life into an intelligible and acceptable narrative — to be capable of narrating oneself as the agent of one's own story.*"
- **Accessibility question raised:** Should the iPad experience work with voice, pointer, or one hand? (Not yet resolved.)
- **iPad form factor:** Idea of building a physical case or environment around the iPad as part of the installation.

# Field visit

- Visited residents at Les Sureaux (Jean and Bernard) — observation of care in a housing community context.
