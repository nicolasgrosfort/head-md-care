# Key decisions

- **Thread (fil) mechanic born:** Watching Muriel present the scénography (15 colored threads connecting all 7 islands), Nico decides to integrate the thread into the game. Hans discovers a thread while digging and follows it underground.
- **Project received very positively** — green light for a highly poetic approach.
- **Cimetière des Rois confirmed as mandatory starting point** — Cassandre & Muriel require the experience to be anchored in Geneva before entering imaginative worlds.

# Feedback — Cassandre & Muriel

- The starting point must be in Geneva.
- Seasonal variation: same loop with environmental variations per season, or distinct scenario per season? (Constraint: no dead leaves in summer.)
- **Non-action as interaction:** How does the player *perform* non-action? Is non-action = non-interaction? What gesture represents wú wéi on a touchscreen?

# Feedback — Douglas & Andreia

- Extremely positive. "Poetic, sensitive, anchored in Geneva." Muriel loved it.
- **Thread as central mechanic:** Very well received — should be explored as the primary interaction, not just a motif.
- **Not necessarily Hans as protagonist:** Could be any cemetery visitor, or no named character at all.
- **Ver de terre (earthworm):** Suggested as a central element. The thread could *be* the earthworm — living connector between underground layers. Earthworm in space as a poetic endpoint.
- **Game references:** *Noby Noby Boy* (worm-like mechanic), *Hohokum* (serpentine, poetic navigation). Strong aesthetic directions.
- **Key reference: *L'Aleph* (Borges):** The yew tree as the Aleph — a point from which all other points are seen simultaneously.

# Borges framework 

- **Thread as fil d'Ariane inversé:** Hans follows the thread *into* the labyrinth, not out. The Minotaur at the center is Hans's own death.
- **Borges' tomb inscription:** "And ne forhtedon na" (Old English: *And they were not afraid*) — the full emotional arc of Hans.
- **Underground as borgesian space:** Biblioteca de Babel (fragments of buried lives), Jardin des sentiers qui bifurquent (4 levels × 4 seasons), Las ruinas circulares (Hans was "dreamed" — liberation, not horror).
- **Narrative spine:**
  ```
  Hans digs near Borges' tomb → finds a red thread → follows it underground
  → traverses 4 levels × 4 seasons → encounters buried lives
  → realizes he was "dreamed" by the cemetery
  → reaches the yew (the Aleph) → lets go of the thread
  → "And ne forhtedon na." Hans disappears.
  ```
