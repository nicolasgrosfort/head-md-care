# Key decisions

- **Team formed:** Nico + Janka (Eryan Chen) working on Scenario 6 — the cemetery.
- **Central metaphor:** The tree as portal between life and death; the leaf as the lifecycle metaphor.

# Key ideas

## Why the cemetery (personal)

- **Nico:** The cemetery is the only place where his ego disappears, where he can be fully present. It paradoxically brings him closer to life. It is where he hears himself think.
- **Janka:** Played in her uncle's cemetery as a child. A calm, tree-filled place for the living, not the dead. A place to release emotions.
- **Synthesis:** The cemetery is a portal to life — an invitation to be alone with oneself, close to one's heart, away from the mind.

## Concepts introduced

- ***Luo Yè Guì Gèn* (落葉歸根):** Introduced by Janka — the Chinese concept that a leaf falls back to nourish the tree it came from. Being buried where one was born.
- **Pando Tree:** The largest living organism — a grove of aspen trees connected by a single underground rhizome. Reference for underground connectivity.
- **Tree burial / Capsula Mundi:** Biodegradable capsule containing ashes buried under a tree, transforming cemeteries into forests. Symbolic: the tree represents eternal life and return to earth.
- **Care paradox (first articulation):** Cleaning gravestones with chemicals keeps the stone pristine but kills the surrounding nature. A tension between two forms of care emerges here.

## Narrative sketch

- **"The Gardener of Souls"** — first working title idea.
- **Central tension:** The gardener breaks the loop of *Luo Yè Guì Gèn* by removing dead leaves. Does tending the cemetery truly care for it — or does it domesticate nature artificially?
- First sketches made: tree/leaf/transition, cemetery as temple, care mind map.
