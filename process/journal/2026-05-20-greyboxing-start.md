# Key decisions

- **Greyboxing in Unity begins** — first spatial prototype of all four levels.

# Group feedback on prototype

- **Scanner idea:** Seeing light from below — the underground radiates upward, as if something alive is down there. Potentially a scanning/revelation mechanic.
- **Cosmos impression:** The prototype gives a sense of a complete cosmology — underground and sky as one continuous whole.
- **Underground and sky made of the same material:** Visual continuity between the underworld and the cosmos reinforces the cycle/loop motif. The underworld is not separate from the heavens — they mirror each other.
- **Split-screen / multiple cameras idea:** Running multiple cameras simultaneously (one per layer), splitting the screen so the player can act in all environments at once. Open question: does this enrich or fragment the experience?
