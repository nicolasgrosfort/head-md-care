# Context

- **With:** Jean and Bernard — retired residents who chose to engage more deeply in the project.
- **Location:** Les Sureaux, cooperative community housing.
- **Focus:** Inclusivity and community living as care.

# Key observations

- **FALC technique** (Facile à Lire et à Comprendre) used for communication: lots of drawings, developed by social educators for people with disabilities. Necessary because not everyone speaks French — or speaks at all.
- **Community specificity:** inclusivity of disability, but also generational inclusion — how different generations cohabit (sociopolitical, philosophical, spiritual, economic dimensions).
- **30–40 residents**, of whom 12 have disabilities. Residents with disabilities typically live with parents (when alive), otherwise with educators or guardians.
- **Few organized activities:** instead, a constant benevolent presence. Simply being there, being among others — no action required.
- **Jean and Bernard:** both retired, chose to give more of themselves to the project. Voluntary self-giving as a particular form of care.
- **CODAH structure:** runs as an association with working groups holding sub-responsibilities. Monthly RIRE meetings (Réunion Informelle Revitalisante).

# Key ideas

- **Care as presence:** the primary act is simply being available — to others, to what is happening. No activity needed.
- **Institutionalization of togetherness:** Jean observes a delegation of being-together to institutions.
  - Before: everyone together.
  - Now: children → daycare, elderly → EMS, disabled → facilities.
- **Care extends beyond people** — to objects, spaces, land, buildings.
- Quote: *"Totalement différent d'un immeuble locatif ordinaire."*
