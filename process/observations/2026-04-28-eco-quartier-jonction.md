# Context

- **Location:** Eco-Quartier Jonction, cooperative apartment building.
- **Format:** Guided visit of the premises.

# Key observations

- **Mini-apartments within apartments:** each resident has their own private cocoon, with all other spaces mutualized. Everything shared except the bed.
- **Balance:** the model feels well-calibrated between individual privacy and collective living.

# Key ideas

- **Personal and collective care in balance:** the structure embodies both self-care (private space) and collective care (shared infrastructure).
- **Mutualisation as a care model:** close to the Japanese capsule hotel model — everything shared except the most intimate space. A mode of living that makes sense and is worth exploring.
- **Care as a path back to cohabitation:** reconnecting to living with others as a way of re-engaging with care practices more broadly.
