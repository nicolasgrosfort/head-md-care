# Context

- **Location:** Cimetière des Rois (Cimetière de Plainpalais), Geneva.
- **Format:** Free roam followed by a guided story creation exercise.

# Key observations

- **Interactive tomb:** a sandbox on a tombstone where visitors can draw, leave objects, or return to childhood.
- **Magic lamp tomb:** a lamp on a grave suggesting the genie of the deceased — echoes Aladdin. What is a genie? The word carries many meanings across many contexts.
- **Trees:** visibly more intense — taller, greener, denser. Feel connected not just to the earth but to the souls below. A secondary network. A second life.
- **Tombs and trees co-evolve:** nature and death seem to coexist and influence each other.

# Key ideas

- **Cemetery as portal:** it sits at the arrival line — an invitation to pause in *doing* and enter *being*. An invitation to feel, which is reserved for the living.
- **Creating a story = linking things together:** putting two things in relation. Example: a butterfly landing on a slate shaped like its wings to die — the other slates become former butterflies.
- **Tree as genie:** for those who take the time to be and inhabit the moment, the tree accomplishes wonders. The tree as portal linking the dead to the living. Tree as crystal ball — to truly look at it is to read the future, because it demands choices.

# Story exercise

Focused on the genie and the trees — positing both as different manifestations of the same thing: access to the self.

*"L'arbre est un génie. À qui prendra le temps, d'être et d'habiter l'instant, accomplira des merveilles. Accomplira l'impossible, à commencer par une rencontre. Avec soi, celui qu'on ne voit plus. Avec ça, cette énergie de vie qui nous aide à cheminer. L'arbre est un portail. Il nous lie aux morts, et de ce fait, nous relie à la vie. L'arbre est une boule de cristal. À qui le regarde vraiment pourra lire le futur, parce qu'il devra faire des choix."*
