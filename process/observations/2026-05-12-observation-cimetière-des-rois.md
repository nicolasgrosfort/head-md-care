# Context

- **Location:** Cimetière des Rois (Cimetière de Plainpalais), Geneva.
- **Format:** Solo visit — meeting with Sandrine and Verena (SEVE) did not take place. Observation conducted while waiting.

# Key observations

- **Entry inscription** on the cemetery's only building:
  > *"Heureux ceux qui meurent au Seigneur. Ils se reposent de leurs travaux et leurs oeuvres les suivent."* — Saint Jean
- **Tombs 724 and 725:** entirely covered in moss, no maintenance. They disappear into the landscape — death becomes progressively invisible, absorbed by the new life growing over it.
- **Hard shadows** when the sun is out: trees and tombs cast patterns onto the ground — temporary, ephemeral marks, like life itself.
- **Path surface:** half gravel, half concrete. Reason unknown.
- **Cemetery map** posted near the entrance building: lists names, tomb numbers, and locations.
- Several squirrels observed with full bushy tails.

# Key ideas

- **Invisible death:** unmaintained tombs blend into the environment — they are no longer seen. Does forgetting follow invisibility?
- **Shadows as ephemeral traces:** the cemetery draws temporary marks on the ground, as fleeting as a life.
