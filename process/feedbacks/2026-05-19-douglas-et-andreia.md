# Context

- **With:** Douglas and Andreia — project review session.

# Positive feedback

- **Overwhelming positive reception** from Muriel and Cassandre: described as poetic, sensitive, and rooted in Geneva.
- **Cimetière des Rois as starting point** — strongly affirmed, must be kept.
- **Thread very well received** — should be kept and explored as the central mechanic.
- **Green light for a fully poetic direction** for the exhibition.

# Key ideas

- **No fixed character needed** — the user is the character. The gardener is not mandatory; a cemetery visitor could work as well.
- **Earthworm (ver de terre) as central figure:** the thread as the worm — a connector between different soil layers, the final form of the thread's journey. The earthworm's work in the soil (digging, creating passages) is significant. Worth exploring further.

# References

- **Aleph** — *Jorge Luis Borges* short story collection. Themes: labyrinths, infinity, death, immortality. Directly relevant.
- **Noby Noby Boy** — game involving a stretching worm-like character.
- **Hohokum** — game with a worm/ribbon-like protagonist. Art direction noted as particularly appealing.
