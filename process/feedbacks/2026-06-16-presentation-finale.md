# Context

- **With:** Cassandre Poirier and her director.

# Positive feedback

- **Art direction fits the exhibition's message** — concepts and positioning around care are aligned with the broader show.
- **Transparency** appreciated as a visual quality.
- **Thread integration** — the idea of connecting Hans's thread from the scenography into the iPad experience is well received.
- **Dream-like atmosphere** works and could be pushed further in the art direction.

# Critical feedback

- **Cimetière des Rois is not identifiable** — the location does not read clearly enough. More visual cues or references to the specific cemetery are needed.
- **Voices proposed as a feedback layer** — Verena's voice (and possibly Guillem's) could give the user information about the consequences of their actions, going beyond visual or musical feedback alone.
- **Interactions removal suggested** by Cassandre and director — not agreed with.

# Open questions

- How to introduce recognizable markers of the Cimetière des Rois without breaking the visual coherence?
- What is the right role for voice — ambient, reactive, or narrative?
- **Keeping interactions:** the suggestion to remove them was rejected. What is the best argument for their necessity?
